# Measures of Dispersion

## Why do we need measures of dispersion?

Imagine two datasets having exactly the same average.

Does that mean both datasets have the same characteristics?

Not necessarily.

Measures of central tendency tell us where the centre of a dataset lies. However, they don't tell us how widely the observations are distributed.

This is where measures of dispersion become important.
